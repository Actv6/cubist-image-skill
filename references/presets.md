# Cubist presets

## Presets

### `fractured-primary` — default

Best for portraits and bold social artwork. Use cobalt blue, vermilion, mustard yellow, chalk white, charcoal black, and muted olive. Add thick dark contours, large irregular planes, split-profile facial construction, and visible brush texture.

### `analytical-ochre`

Best for architecture, objects, and dense compositions. Use ochre, umber, smoke gray, muted olive, and small blue accents. Break forms into many translucent facets with shallow depth and overlapping viewpoints.

### `paper-collage`

Best for posters and graphic compositions. Use torn paper, newsprint-like fragments without readable text, painted shapes, charcoal lines, and flat overlapping color blocks. Keep edges tactile and imperfect.

### `blue-expression`

Best for solitary figures and reflective scenes. Use ultramarine, Prussian blue, slate, pale cyan, and small warm highlights. Keep the Cubist geometry strong while making the mood quiet and emotionally heavy.

### `rose-stage`

Best for groups, performers, and gentle portraits. Use dusty rose, terracotta, sand, muted coral, pale blue, and charcoal. Keep the geometry fractured but soften the contours and emotional tone.

## Subject additions

- **Portrait:** retain one figure and dominant pose; split face across front and profile viewpoints; offset eyes and mouth; keep hair or clothing as a recognizable color anchor.
- **Architecture:** retain skyline silhouette and building count approximately; replace correct perspective with overlapping elevations, angled window rhythms, and fractured glass planes.
- **Street/object:** retain the dominant arrows, object silhouettes, or directional rhythm; convert surface markings into abstract graphic blocks without readable text.
- **Landscape:** retain horizon direction and main land/water masses; compress distance into stacked angular planes.

## Intensity corrections

- If too photographic: request larger planes, more displaced features, flatter depth, and multiple viewpoints.
- If unrecognizable: restore the dominant silhouette and one identifying color/object anchor without reducing the geometric language.
- If cluttered: reduce the palette to five colors and simplify the background into broad interlocking planes.
- If it resembles a known artwork: change palette, pose, spatial arrangement, and plane rhythm; require a clearly original composition.
